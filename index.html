About

I am a Member of Technical Staff at Perplexity, where I work on computer-use agent RL training.

I earned my M.S. and bachelor's degrees at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign (UIUC), where I worked closely with Prof. Minjia Zhang on PyTorch compiler optimization.

I also contribute to open source, including SGLang, Miles, TransformerEngine and PyTorch.

Research interest: efficient computer-use agent training.
